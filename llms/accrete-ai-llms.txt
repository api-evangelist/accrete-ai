# Accrete

> Accrete is a dual-use enterprise and government AI company (founded 2017, New York) that licenses Expert AI Agents built on its Knowledge Engine Platform. The platform turns human domain expertise and unstructured data into autonomously constructed knowledge graphs its agents reason over. Accrete does not publish a public developer API, SDK, or machine-readable API contract — products are licensed and delivered directly or through partner marketplaces such as Snowflake.

Provenance: generated 2026-08-02 by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from apis.yml and this repository's artifacts. Accrete publishes no /llms.txt of its own (https://www.accrete.ai/llms.txt returned 404 on 2026-08-02).

## Products

- [Knowledge Engine Platform](https://www.accrete.ai/ke-platform): Core infrastructure that unifies fragmented enterprise data into dynamic knowledge graphs for agent reasoning with long-term memory.
- [Argus](https://www.accrete.ai/argus): Government agent line for supply-chain FOCI risk, narrative intelligence, and information operations.
- [Argus Supply Chain](https://www.accrete.ai/argus-supply-chain): Detection of hidden ownership ties and foreign-ownership-control-influence risk in supplier networks.
- [Argus Cognitive Advantage](https://www.accrete.ai/argus-cognitive-advantage): Narrative and influence analysis for information operations.
- [Argus for Law Enforcement](https://www.accrete.ai/argus-law-enforcement): Investigative application of the Argus agent line.
- [Nebula ITSM](https://www.accrete.ai/nebula-itsi): Enterprise IT service management and change-risk prediction agent, shipped as a Snowflake Native App.
- [Nebula Social](https://www.accrete.ai/nebula-social): Social narrative tracking, brand protection, and influence detection.
- [Knowledge Functions](https://www.accrete.ai/knowledge-functions): The reusable functional building blocks of the Knowledge Engine.
